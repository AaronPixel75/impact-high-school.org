Your Path to Excellence

A structured journey from enrollment to graduation

Foundation (S1 - S2)

Building strong roots in core Sciences, Mathematics, and Languages. Students explore a wide range of subjects to discover their passions.

Focus (S3 - S4)

Introduction of optional subjects and intensified preparation for UCE examinations. Practical lab work becomes central.

Specialization (S5 - S6)

Advanced Level studies. Students select specific combinations (Science or Arts) tailored to their future career goals.

University Placement

Career guidance, university application support, and scholarship recommendations to ensure the next big step.
